💡 Help & User Guide

About this Portal

This portal shows faculty and student performance for the PW Maharashtra Region. Data is pulled live from the shared Google Sheets. Everything you see changes based on the filters you pick.

🏠 Home — Filters

The filter bar applies to everything on the Home screen (KPIs, tables, graphs):

  • Center — only the selected center's batches/students.
  • Class / Stream — e.g. Dropper NEET, Dropper JEE.
  • Batch — a single batch, e.g. 60-YN511CP.
  • Faculty — only the faculty member's own batches.
  • From / To Date — only tests between these dates.

Use Reset to clear everything back to All.

📊 Home — KPI Cards

  • Center — which center(s) you are viewing.
  • Total Batches — batches that had at least one test.
  • Total Students — students who gave at least one test.
  • Total Faculty — faculty mapped to the visible batches.
  • Avg Score — average of every test's percentage (marks %).
  • Average Students — students whose average is within ±5% of the overall average (the % below = share of total students).
  • Absent Students — students who gave no test in the selected scope (the % below = share of total students).

🏆 Home — Topper & Bottom Students

Lists the students with the highest / lowest average percentage.

  • Top / Bottom <number> — type any number (1-500) to see that many students; default is 10.
  • Tests — how many tests that student gave.
  • Avg % — average of the student's test percentages. Subject columns show the latest test's subject marks.
  • Download (⬇) exports the currently visible list.

🏆 Home — Best / Bottom Batch

The batch with the highest / lowest average percentage, plus its top 3 students.

❌ Home — Absent Students

Students whose batch had a test but who gave no test in the selected scope.

  • Papers Not Given — how many of the batch's tests they missed.
  • Name / stream come from the Students sheet.

📊 Home — Batch-wise Subject Average

For every batch: its overall average percentage plus each subject's share.

  • Subject % = that subject's marks ÷ total score × 100, averaged across the batch's tests.
  • A NEET batch shows Maths as 0% because NEET papers have no Maths.

📈 Home — Subject Performance Graph

Per test date, the average of each student's subject share (subject marks ÷ score × 100).

  • Hover any point to see the date, the average score of that test and each subject's %.
  • Faculty users only see their own subjects.

🔍 Student — Single Lookup

Enter a registration number and press Search to see that student's performance graph and full test history (date, type, total, score, %, rank).

📚 Batches

List of batches with their subjects, student count, faculty count and average score.

  • Filters: Subject, Faculty, Search.
  • Click View on a batch to open its detail page: Toppers / Average / Needs Improvement / Absent cards plus the full student list with ranks.

👩‍🎓 Faculty

List of faculty with their batches, subjects and centers.

  • Filters: Center, Search.
  • Faculty accounts only see their own batches and subjects everywhere in the portal.

🎯 Students

List of all students with tests given, average score and best subject.

  • Filters: Batch, Sort By (score / name / tests), Search.
  • Click a student to open their detail page: stats, subject bars and test history.

🧮 How Calculations Work

  • Student Avg % = average of the student's test percentages (marks % column).
  • Batch Avg % = average of all its students' test percentages.
  • Subject % = subject marks ÷ total score × 100 (per test, averaged per student).
  • Tests = tests with a score above zero.
  • Rank = position by score within the test / batch.
  • Average Students = students within ±5% of the overall average score.
